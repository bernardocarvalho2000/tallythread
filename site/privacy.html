This site is run by Bernardo Carvalho, based in Portugal ("we"). We keep data collection to a minimum.

What this site collects

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Gumroad. When you buy, Gumroad collects your payment details and email address under Gumroad's privacy policy. We never see your full card details. Emails

We only email buyers about their purchase and product updates (for example, updated fee rates). We do not sell or share your email address.
