Etsy fees stack up quietly

A single sale can carry a listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee, a payment processing fee, sometimes a regulatory operating fee, and sometimes a 12–15% Offsite Ads fee. They come out of your payout as separate lines, so the number you see in your bank account is hard to connect back to any one order.

Add materials, packaging and shipping labels, and many sellers honestly don't know which products make money and which barely break even.

Example: a $25 item with $5 shipping (US)

  • Etsy fees: $3.30 (listing, transaction, processing)
  • Item and shipping costs: $10.50
  • What you keep: $16.20 — a 54% margin
  • Same order with a 15% Offsite Ads sale: $11.70
